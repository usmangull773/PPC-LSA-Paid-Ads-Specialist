01

What we found

Your brief says it clearly: paid ads is more than launching campaigns. Across a multi-client agency, the work that moves ROI is deciding what counts as a qualified lead for each client, then making Google Ads and LSA optimize toward that signal instead of raw volume.

Bidding follows whatever conversion it is fed

High impact

Smart bidding and Performance Max chase the conversion action they are given. If a 10 second call counts the same as a signed consult, the account learns to buy cheap, low intent calls. Fixing the signal is the first lever on every account.

What wastes budget

  • Every call counted, regardless of duration
  • Form fills and page views weighted the same
  • No feedback on which leads were signed or booked
  • Broad match running without a search term review

What works

  • Call conversions with a minimum duration
  • Micro conversions set to secondary, not bid on
  • Booked or signed outcomes imported back as offline conversions
  • Weekly negatives from real search terms

LSA placement is earned outside the bid

High impact

Local Services Ads charge per lead, and ranking leans heavily on profile quality and responsiveness. A complete, verified profile that answers fast can beat a higher bid.

Week 1

Tracking audit on every assigned account

Check conversion actions, set call minimum duration, move micro conversions to secondary and set up offline import of booked or signed leads.

Reason: bidding only improves once it is told which leads matter.

2
Week 1 to 2

LSA profile and lead hygiene

Review verification, service areas, job types, hours and review flow. Start marking leads as booked and flagging invalid ones for credit.

Reason: LSA ranking and cost per lead depend on profile quality and responsiveness.

3
Week 2 to 3

Search and Performance Max restructure

Group campaigns by service or practice area, add negatives from search terms and shift budget toward the ad groups with the lowest cost per qualified lead.

Reason: the same budget goes further when it sits behind the services that convert.

4
Week 3 to 4

One reporting template for all clients

A Looker Studio dashboard per client showing spend, qualified leads, cost per qualified lead and LSA lead status, in the same layout every time.

Reason: account managers can read any client in minutes and answer client questions without digging.

5
Week 4

Weekly working rhythm with your team

Weekly change log and next actions posted to your task board, plus a short monthly summary each account manager can forward to the client.

Reason: clean handoffs keep accounts moving without extra meetings.
